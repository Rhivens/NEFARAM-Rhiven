# Eleanor — Gameplay Profile

> **Purpose:** decision aid for evaluating NEFARAM-Rhiven mods  
> **Current baseline:** NEFARAM-Rhiven 17.3.8 / `Eleanor Stable 17.3.8`  
> **Status:** living reference document  
> **Scope:** gameplay priorities, combat style, follower usage, transformations, equipment, adult gameplay preferences and mod relevance

---

## 1. Why this document exists

This sheet is not intended as a roleplay biography.

Its main purpose is to help evaluate whether a candidate mod is:

- central to Eleanor's actual playstyle;
- useful but secondary;
- situational;
- redundant;
- irrelevant;
- or actively contrary to the intended playthrough.

This avoids spending too much time validating features Eleanor will never use.

---

## 2. Core gameplay rules

### Transformations

Eleanor does **not** play as:

- Vampire;
- Werewolf.

These paths are outside the normal gameplay scope.

### Consequence for mod evaluation

Mods whose main value depends on Vampire or Werewolf gameplay are normally:

- low priority;
- optional;
- or out of scope.

Compatibility patches may still be kept if technically harmless and required by the stack, but dedicated gameplay validation is not a priority unless a mod forces those states.

---

## 3. Follower philosophy

Eleanor is a predominantly solo character.

### Normal behavior

- Travels alone approximately **95% of the time**.
- Uses a follower mainly when a quest explicitly requires one.
- Otherwise, followers are left/stored in Eleanor's properties or assigned homes.

### Consequence for framework evaluation

Follower-management mods are useful primarily for:

- storing followers safely;
- assigning homes/properties;
- recovering/managing followers when required;
- keeping quest followers functional;
- avoiding follower-state corruption.

Low-value features for Eleanor include:

- permanent large parties;
- tactical formations;
- complex squad combat;
- multi-follower combat optimization;
- elaborate party-command systems.

This should weigh heavily in any future **NFF vs Horde** evaluation.

## 4. Combat style

Eleanor's standard combat setup is:

- **Right hand:** Katana
- **Left hand:** Destruction magic

She is therefore a hybrid **one-handed melee + spellcasting** character.

### Important compatibility targets

Mods affecting the following deserve real gameplay testing:

- one-handed animations;
- katana animation sets;
- blocking behavior;
- left-hand casting;
- dual behavior between weapon and spell;
- dodge systems;
- animation state transitions;
- third-person combat movement;
- camera behavior during combat;
- weapon/spell equip transitions.

### Test priority

Any animation/combat mod should be tested specifically with:

> Katana right hand + Destruction spell left hand

rather than with generic sword-and-shield or dual-wield assumptions.

## 5. Current relevance framework

When reviewing a candidate mod, use the following priority logic.

### High relevance

A mod is highly relevant when it directly improves:

- solo exploration;
- Eleanor's katana/magic combat;
- survival/needs/hygiene if retained;
- questing;
- immersion;
- reputation/social reactions;
- adult gameplay systems Eleanor actually uses;
- outfit/armor systems;
- home/property management;
- safe follower storage;
- quality-of-life for a very large MO2 setup.

### Medium relevance

A mod may be useful when it improves:

- occasional quest followers;
- specialized side activities;
- optional roleplay systems;
- isolated quests;
- situational adult content;
- visual/animation improvements.

### Low relevance

A mod is normally low priority when centered on:

- Vampire gameplay;
- Werewolf gameplay;
- permanent follower parties;
- large tactical follower squads;
- systems Eleanor rarely encounters;
- mechanics already covered cleanly by the current build.

## 10. Armor, clothing and wardrobe

Eleanor maintains a **large wardrobe** and makes active use of outfit-management tools.

### Clothing philosophy

- Very suggestive outfits are welcome.
- Light armor is preferred.
- Medium/intermediate protection is acceptable.
- Heavy armor is **not** part of Eleanor's playstyle.
- Jewelry is important: necklaces, rings and decorative accessories.

### Contextual clothing

Eleanor changes clothing depending on context:

- **At home:** nightwear / lingerie-style homewear.
- **In cities:** civilian clothing / city outfits.
- **Dangerous quests:** armor.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

High relevance:

- outfit managers;
- wardrobe systems;
- armor/clothing packs with many interchangeable pieces;
- light armor collections;
- jewelry/accessory packs;
- context-aware outfit switching;
- home/city/adventure outfit presets.

Low relevance:

- heavy-armor-only packs;
- crafting-intensive armor systems;
- mods whose value depends mainly on armor progression through smithing.

## 11. Survival and travel habits

Eleanor uses survival mechanics actively.

### Managed needs

- hunger;
- thirst;
- sleep;
- cold;
- hygiene.

### Hygiene

Eleanor bathes frequently, often **several times per day**.

Therefore hygiene/bathing systems are a recurring gameplay loop rather than a cosmetic detail.

### Wilderness behavior

Eleanor likes to:

- create campfires;
- pitch tents;
- rest in the wilderness;
- use camps as temporary safe points.

She generally avoids traveling at night when possible.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

High relevance:

- SunHelm/needs integration;
- bathing/hygiene systems;
- camping/tent/fire systems;
- survival widgets;
- weather/cold readability;
- rest/camp QoL.

Low relevance:

- redundant hunger/thirst frameworks;
- survival systems that add heavy micromanagement without meaningful benefit.

## 12. Homes, properties and follower storage

Eleanor tries to acquire **as many properties as possible across Skyrim**.

### Property strategy

Properties serve as:

- safe hubs distributed across the map;
- wardrobe/outfit locations;
- follower assignment locations;
- rest points;
- family housing.

Followers are distributed among Eleanor's homes rather than traveling permanently with her.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

High relevance:

- player-home mods;
- property acquisition;
- home-management systems;
- follower home assignment;
- reliable follower relocation/storage;
- geographically distributed safe houses.

This reinforces the importance of evaluating follower frameworks for **home assignment reliability**, not party combat.

## 14. Crafting philosophy

Eleanor does **very little crafting**.

### Weapons

Her legendary Katana is obtained through **AddItemMenu / item-browser tooling** rather than crafted through progression.

### Clothing

Most city outfits and zero-armor clothing are also obtained through AddItemMenu-style tooling.

### Armor

Combat armor is usually acquired organically from defeated enemies and assembled from found pieces.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

High relevance:

- AddItemMenu / Modex / Item Vault-style tools;
- lootable armor integration;
- outfit management.

Low relevance:

- deep smithing progression;
- crafting perk overhauls;
- material-grind armor systems;
- mods whose main value is crafting recipes.

## 16. Quest philosophy

Eleanor's preferred quest types include:

- adult/NSFW quests;
- Dragonborn/main heroic quest content;
- small secondary quests.

### Quest-management habit

Eleanor normally keeps **one active quest at a time**.

The usual pattern is:

1. activate one quest in the journal;
2. complete or progress it;
3. then deliberately activate the next one.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

High relevance:

- compact self-contained side quests;
- modular quest packs;
- small quest mods with limited cross-dependencies;
- adult quest content;
- mods that do not flood the journal.

Potentially lower relevance:

- huge interconnected quest frameworks;
- mods that automatically start many parallel quests;
- systems that spam objectives or force multiple active questlines.

This reinforces the Rhiven preference for **several small, contained quest mods over one giant invasive framework**, when practical.

## 17. Defeat / surrender / capture profile

Eleanor uses surrender as a deliberate survival mechanic when combat is going badly.

### Typical loop

- surrender when health becomes critically low;
- use the resulting pacification/event state to survive;
- regain agency afterward;
- resolve the encounter and continue the quest.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

High relevance:

- surrender systems;
- defeat frameworks;
- reliable pacification;
- clean recovery of player control;
- post-event recovery;
- systems that do not permanently derail quests.

Low relevance:

- punitive defeat systems with little player agency;
- long unrecoverable capture states;
- systems that make surrender less viable than simply reloading.

For Eleanor, defeat mechanics are primarily a survival alternative, not a punishment simulator.

## 20. Skyrim Unbound starting conditions

Eleanor's intended new-game baseline is deliberately restrictive.

### Starting state

- clothing: rags only;
- weapons: none;
- gold: none;
- supplies: one water flask.

### Design intent

The opening phase should feel precarious and force reliance on:

- social/economic improvisation;
- prostitution;
- begging;
- theft;
- scavenging;
- enemy loot;
- camps and properties when available.

### Mod-evaluation consequence

Avoid candidate mods that trivialize this start by granting:

- powerful starter gear;
- large amounts of gold;
- free armor or weapons;
- easy bypasses for the intended survival/economy loop.
